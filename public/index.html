◆ The Insight

The answer already has a name.
Extended Producer Responsibility.

01
The principle
"The one who profits from placing a product on the market must share in what happens to it at end-of-life."
02
Global adoption
40+ countries operate mature EPR schemes. Germany, France, UK, Italy, Japan, Korea — recovery rates 60–95%.
03
Egyptian foundation
Law 202/2020 · Article 17 already authorises WMRA to delegate EPR schemes to specialised entities. The legal door is open.
EPR is not a tax. Financial proceeds must be allocated to the implementation of the EPR scheme itself — collection, treatment, awareness, and audit.
◆ The Design

Three layers · One system

Mizan is proposed to operate through a governance structure that no single actor controls — a design pattern followed by Erion (Italy), Ecosystem (France), Recolight (UK), and Stiftung EAR (Germany).

01
MIZAN ALLIANCE · Non-Profit Governance
Founded by the Federation of Egyptian Industries via ECO-FEI · governed by producers through the Engineering + ICT Chambers · zero-dividend clause. Represents obligated producers.
10%
02
MIZAN OPERATIONS · ChemiCan
Platform · calculation engine · chain-of-custody · certificate generation · Microsoft Azure with Egyptian data residency. Long-term exclusive operating contract proposed.
30%
03
FIELD LAYER · Open Competitive Network
WMRA-licensed collectors + treatment facilities · selection through the algorithmic MIZAN INDEX · no exclusivity · any licensed operator can compete.
60%
